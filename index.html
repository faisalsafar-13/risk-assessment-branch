2Risk register

The controls are what a well-run maktab should have in place. For each hazard, say whether the controls are in place, rate the risk now, and add any extra action with an owner. Mark hazards that don't apply to your site as N/A.

How to rate
Likelihood1 Unlikely: could happen, but rarely · 2 Possible: could happen this term · 3 Likely: expected to happen
Severity1 Minor: first aid only · 2 Serious: hospital treatment · 3 Major: life-changing injury or death
RiskLikelihood × Severity. 1–2 Low: keep controls, review each term · 3–4 Medium: add controls within 4 weeks · 6–9 High: act before the next session, or stop the activity
